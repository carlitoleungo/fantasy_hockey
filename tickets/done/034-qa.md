### Notes

**1. Goalie Assists no longer render (the consequence the Engineer flagged). Judged: within the
ticket, zero observed impact, worth a follow-up item.**

Yahoo tags Assists as `stat_group == "offense"` even though it applies to goalies too
(`data/client.py:107-108` comments on exactly this). Under the `stat_group` partition the ticket
prescribed, the Assists column is absent from the Goalies table.

I measured the impact rather than reasoning about it. Across both team fragments, **260 cells per
fragment disappeared between the pre- and post-change renders, and every single one of them held
`0.0` or `0.00`** (`dropped cells whose value was NOT zero: count: 0`, both team keys). All 8
goalies in the demo snapshot have no offense stats at all in `lastmonth_stats` — Yahoo returned
none for them — so their Assists cells rendered `0.0` before and are simply gone now. No number a
manager could have read changed. I cannot confirm the same holds for every live league, because I
have no live session; `get_players_lastmonth_stats` would surface a goalie assist if Yahoo returned
one.

This does not violate an AC: AC1 is satisfied, AC5's "spot-checked value matches" holds for all 535
common cells, and the ticket explicitly directed partitioning on `stat_group` and sanctioned Option
A. Suggest the PM/Reviewer log a `Type: quality` improvements item if goalie tables should also
carry the shared offense categories — I have not logged one myself, since it is a product call, not
a defect.
